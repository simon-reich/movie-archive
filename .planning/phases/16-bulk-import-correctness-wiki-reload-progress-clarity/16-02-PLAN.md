<objective>
Distinguish "stopped early" from "fully completed" in the wiki-reload batch progress UI
(WR-02, flagged in 14-REVIEW.md, deferred from Phase 14) and thread the real 3-value backend
outcome (SUCCESS/NOT_FOUND/FAILED) through the per-movie history list instead of collapsing it
to a 2-state SUCCESS/FAILED display.

Purpose: today `WikiReloadProgressService.complete()` always reports `(total, total, true, ...)`
regardless of whether Stop was clicked mid-run, so a stopped run's final panel misleadingly
looks 100% done — and then vanishes entirely once `settings.vue`'s `v-if` guard hides it. The
per-movie history also hides whether "no Wikipedia article exists" (expected, not an error)
happened vs. a real fetch failure.
Output: `ProgressState` gains a `stopped` field, `complete()` reports the real last-published
processed count and an accurate `stopped` flag; the frontend `WikiReloadProgress` type and
`settings.vue` thread the new field through a status-text computed, an extended visibility
guard, and a 3-state history-list icon/label.
</objective>
